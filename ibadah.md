# Ibadah Research

## Research Question

Most translations render:

```text
عبد (abd)
عبادة (ibadah)
اعبدوا (u'budu)
```

as:

```text
slave
servant
worship
adoration
```

This study investigates whether the Qur'an consistently uses these terms in the sense of worship, or whether they describe accepting authority, guidance, and direction.

---

## Working LOS Hypothesis

LOS reconstruction:

```text
ع = intellect

ب = desire / intent

د = record

ة = permission
```

Working formula:

```text
Accepting, recording and permitting
that which the intellect recognizes.
```

Short expression:

```text
Ibadah

=

allowing a chosen authority
to guide one's actions.
```

---

## Tested Verses

### 16:36

```text
Serve Allah
and avoid Taghut.
```

Observation:

```text
The contrast appears to be:

Allah

vs

Taghut

as competing authorities.
```

---

### 17:23

```text
Do not perform ibadah
except for Him.
```

Observation:

```text
The verse is immediately followed by
instructions concerning parents.

This suggests that ibadah is connected
to authority and conduct.
```

---

### 19:44

```text
Do not serve Shaytan.
```

Observation:

```text
The issue does not appear to be
ritual worship of Shaytan.

The verse may instead concern
following or obeying Shaytan.
```

---

### 36:60-61

```text
Do not perform ibadah for Shaytan.

Perform ibadah for Me.
```

Observation:

```text
One of the strongest test cases.

The contrast appears to concern
which authority guides one's actions.
```

---

### 45:23

```text
The one who takes his desire
as his god.
```

Observation:

```text
This verse suggests that something
other than Allah can receive practical authority
over a person's life.
```

---

### 51:56

```text
I created jinn and mankind
only so that they may ya'budun.
```

Research question:

```text
Does this refer primarily
to ritual worship,

or

to living under Divine guidance?
```

---

### 98:5

```text
liya'budu Allah

↓

salat

↓

zakaat
```

Observation:

```text
Ibadah appears before
salat and zakaat.

This may indicate that ibadah
is the broader principle
from which salat and zakaat emerge.
```
