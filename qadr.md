# QADR Research Dossier
## LOS Investigation of قَدْر / قَدَر

---

# Research Objective

This research investigates the Qur'anic meaning of:

```text
قَدْر
(Qadr)

قَدَر
(Qadar)

قَدَّرَ
(Qaddara)
```

The purpose is to determine whether the Qur'anic usage primarily refers to:

```text
fate

destiny

predestination
```

or whether the concept represents a process of:

```text
determination

registration

allocation

establishment
```

The strongest operational image produced by the LOS sequence is:

```text
observer

↓

recorder

↓

reviewer
```

Similar to:

```text

archivist

```

```text

```

