# Research Objective

This investigation seeks to determine the Qur'anic meaning of:

```text
صبر
(Sabr)
```

The objective is to derive meaning from Qur'anic context rather than inherited assumptions.

# Initial Question

Most translations render sabr as:

```text
patience

endurance

perseverance
```

The question investigated was:

```text
Is sabr passive waiting?

Or does it describe
an active state?
```
